01 — ABOUT

Geography, landscapes & the ideas behind my research.

I am a PhD researcher in Geography at Savitribai Phule Pune University, working at the intersection of geomorphology, geospatial techniques and sustainable land and water resource management.

02 — WHO I AM

Studying landscapes through geography.

My academic work is rooted in physical geography, particularly geomorphology and the study of landscape processes.

I am interested in understanding how landforms, drainage systems, terrain and environmental processes interact, and how geographical knowledge can contribute to more sustainable management of natural resources.

My approach combines geographical thinking with spatial technologies, field observations and quantitative analysis.
