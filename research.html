Understanding landscapes, resources & environmental systems.

My research focuses on geomorphology and the use of geographical and geospatial approaches to understand landforms, terrain, drainage systems and natural resources.

01 — PHD RESEARCH

Geomorphic Assessment for Sustainable Land and Water Resources Management of Northeast Aravalli Hills of Rajasthan

PHD RESEARCH

My PhD research examines the geomorphic characteristics of the Northeast Aravalli Hills of Rajasthan and their relationship with land and water resources.

The broader research objective is to contribute to a better geographical understanding of the landscape and support approaches to sustainable land and water resource management.

RAJASTHAN • INDIA
